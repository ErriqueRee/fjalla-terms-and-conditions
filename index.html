General Terms and Conditions for Artists

General Terms and Conditions (T&C) for fjalla — T&C for Artists

§ 2Subject Matter / Services

2.1 fjalla enables Artists to upload audio content ("Tracks"), musical works, recordings, and short videos ("Reels"), including the required metadata, for publication and availability within fjalla's streaming catalogue.

2.2 fjalla pays Artists a usage-based remuneration ("Artist Payout") for streams in accordance with the applicable payout model shown in the Artist Dashboard.

2.3 fjalla may modify the Platform's technical scope, features, formats, availability, user interface, monetisation models, or payout systems at any time, provided such changes are reasonable for Artists.

§ 3Registration / Artist Account / Identity

3.1 Use of the Platform requires the creation of an Artist Account.

3.2 Artists warrant that all information provided during registration and use (name, address, banking details, tax details, identity details, etc.) is complete, current, and truthful.

3.3 fjalla may request identity verification (KYC), tax information, and proof of rights ownership at any time. fjalla may restrict or suspend accounts until verification is completed.

3.4 fjalla may suspend accounts immediately without prior notice if misuse, fraud, rights violations, or false information is suspected.

§ 4Upload Process / Metadata Obligations / Quality

4.1 Artists must provide all required metadata completely and correctly, including but not limited to:

  • Track title, ISRC, artist name, contributors
  • Composers, authors, producers, featured artists, split information
  • Label/publisher information (if applicable)
  • Cover art rights
  • Content warnings and any mandatory disclosures

4.2 Artists acknowledge that incorrect or incomplete metadata may result in streams being misattributed and may lead to infringement of third-party rights.

4.3 fjalla is not obligated to verify metadata. Full responsibility remains with the Artist.

§ 5Mandatory Requirement: 100% Independent / Rights Chain / No Collecting Societies

5.1 fjalla accepts only content from Artists who are 100% independent.

5.2 "100% independent" means in particular that the Artist:

  • owns or fully controls all rights in the master recordings and underlying works,
  • has no exclusive or conflicting agreements with labels, distributors, publishers, producers, managers, or other third parties,
  • has not assigned any rights (even partially) to third parties in a way that conflicts with exploitation through fjalla.

5.3 fjalla does not cooperate worldwide with collecting societies (e.g., GEMA, PRS, ASCAP, BMI, SACEM, SIAE, SUISA, SOCAN, CISAC member societies, etc.), record labels, publishers, or collective rights management organisations.

5.4 Artists may upload content only if such content is not subject to exclusive representation or claims by a collecting society or publisher if such claims could result in liabilities for fjalla.

5.5 If an Artist is nevertheless a member of a collecting society, the Artist warrants that:

  • fjalla will not be subject to any obligations arising from such membership,
  • the Artist will handle and pay all claims arising from such membership independently,
  • the Artist will fully indemnify fjalla.

  • cover art / artwork / trademark rights
  • names, logos, samples, loops, beats, voices, AI-generated voices, remixes

6.2 The Artist warrants that:

  • no samples or third-party materials are used without valid licensing,
  • no third-party rights are infringed,
  • no personal rights are violated,
  • there are no pending legal disputes or claims relating to the rights.

6.3 The Artist further warrants that the content does not violate any applicable laws, youth protection laws, or public policy.

9.3 Payouts will be made only after reaching a minimum payout threshold and after successful identity and tax verification.

9.4 fjalla may withhold taxes, fees, reserves, chargebacks, payment provider fees, or legally required deductions.

10.1 fjalla may at any time remove, block, disable, freeze, or monetarily suspend content, in particular if:

  • legal or regulatory measures are threatened.

10.2 fjalla may withhold payouts or recover amounts already paid ("Clawback") if:

  • third-party claims arise or are asserted,

10.3 The Artist must repay clawed-back amounts within 14 days of written request. fjalla may offset such claims against future payouts.
